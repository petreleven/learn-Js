
## Challenge 1: The Gamer Level-Up

### The Lore

You just defeated the final boss in *Pixel Quest*, and the game enters "Double XP Mode!" Your character's screen flashes neon green as your points start multiplying. You need to build the game's reward screen to calculate and display the final score.

### The Task

Write a function called `doubleXP` that takes one argument called `points`. Inside the function, multiply the points by 2 and print it to the console with a victory message.

* **Example Output if they pass in `50`:** `"You leveled up! Your new XP is: 100"`

### Step-by-Step Guide

* **Step 1:** Define a new function and give it the name `doubleXP`.
* **Step 2:** Inside the parentheses `()`, create a single placeholder (argument) named `points`. This will hold the player's score.
* **Step 3:** Open the function's body using curly braces `{}`.
* **Step 4:** Inside the braces, create a new variable that multiplies `points` by `2`.
* **Step 5:** Use `console.log()` to print out your final message, mixing your text with that new multiplied number.

### The Task

* **Example Output if they pass in `"Mega"` and `"Hamster"`:** `"Look out! It's Mega Hamster!"`

### Step-by-Step Guide

* **Step 1:** Define a function named `makeHeroName`.
* **Step 2:** Inside the parentheses, create two arguments separated by a comma: `adjective` and `animal`.
* **Step 3:** Open the function's body with curly braces.
* **Step 4:** Use `console.log()` to glue your words together. You will smash together the text `"Look out! It's "`, the `adjective`, a space, and the `animal`.

## Challenge 5: The Pet Age Calculator

### The Lore

Your dog is sitting next to you, looking incredibly wise. You start wondering: if your dog went to school and had a job, how old would they actually be in "dog years"? You decide to code a calculator to find out the truth.

### The Task

Write a function called `dogYears` that takes an argument called `humanAge`. Since 1 dog year is roughly 7 human years, multiply the age by 7 and log the result to the screen.

* **Example Output if they pass in `10`:** `"In dog years, your pet is: 70"`

### Step-by-Step Guide

* **Step 1:** Define a function named `dogYears`.
* **Step 2:** Inside the parentheses, add one argument named `humanAge`.
* **Step 3:** Open the function's body with curly braces.
* **Step 4:** Take the `humanAge` argument and multiply it by `7` using the `*` symbol.
* **Step 5:** Use `console.log()` to display the final calculated dog age to the screen.
