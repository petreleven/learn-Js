**The Mission:** The zombies are getting close! We need an alarm that we can trigger anytime just by calling its name. Create a function called `soundAlarm`. Inside the function, make it print "WARNING: ZOMBIES AT THE GATE!" to the console and print "RUN TAKE YOUR BAGS!".

**Check your work:** Call your function once at the bottom of your code to make sure the alarm actually rings!
