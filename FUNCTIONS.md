## 🚨 The Perimeter Alarm 🚨
**The Mission:** The zombies are getting close! We need an alarm that we can trigger anytime just by calling its name. Create a function called `soundAlarm`. Inside the function, make it print "WARNING: ZOMBIES AT THE GATE!" to the console and print "RUN TAKE YOUR BAGS!".

**Check your work:** Call your function once at the bottom of your code to make sure the alarm actually rings!

## 🎒 The Gear Checker 🎒
**The Mission:** You have a list called `backpack` with "Water", "Map", and "Flashlight". Sometimes we forget what we're carrying! Create a function called `checkGear`. Inside the function, use `console.log` to print your `backpack` list so you can see your supplies.

**Check your work:** Add a new item to your list using `.push("Snack")`, then call your `checkGear()` function to see if the snack shows up in your bag!

## 🕺 The Victory Dance 🕺
**The Mission:** You survived the night! To celebrate, you need a function that performs a three-step dance. Create a function called `victoryDance`. Inside the function, use three different `console.log` lines to print:
1. "Slide to the left!"
2. "Spin around!"
3. "Moonwalk!"

**Check your work:** Call the `victoryDance()` function. You should see all three moves appear in the console in the right order!
