## 🧟 The Zombie Survival Squad 🧟
The Mission: You start with a list called squad that only has two people: "Me" and "Bestie". A group of three more survivors just ran up to your gate! Use .push() three times to add "Scientist", "Robot", and "Pizza Guy" to the end of your team.

Check your work: Print the list to the console to make sure you have 5 survivors ready to fight!

## 🕹️ The Player One Power-Up 🍄
The Mission: You have a list called players with four names in it. In this game, only the person at the very front of the line gets the Golden Mushroom. Use [0] to grab the name of the first player and store it in a variable called winner.

Check your work: Log a message like: "The Golden Mushroom goes to: " plus your winner variable.

## 🕵️ The Double Agent Swap 🕶️
The Mission: You have a list of heroes. But wait—there is a "Spy" hiding at index 2! Use .splice() to perform a secret swap. You need to remove that 1 spy and put a "Super Dog" in that exact spot instead.

Check your work: If you print your list and the "Spy" is gone but the "Super Dog" is sitting in the middle of the heroes, you nailed it!

## 🧹 The Messy Closet Cleanup 👕
The Mission: Your closet list is a mess. It looks like this: ["Shirt", "Old Socks", "Hat"].

First, use .splice() to get rid of those "Old Socks" at index 1.

Next, use .push() to add a "Cool Cape" to the end of the list.

Finally, use [0] to see what your favorite shirt is.

Check your work: Your final list should only have 3 items, and "Old Socks" should be in the trash!
