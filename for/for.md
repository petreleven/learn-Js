

## 📢 Mission: The Morning Roll Call
**The Mission:** You need to make sure everyone is awake and accounted for. Use a `for` loop to go through your `squad` list (the one with 5 people) and print a message for each person.

**The Goal:**
* Create a `for` loop that starts at `0` and runs for the `.length` of the squad.
* Inside the loop, print: **"Survivor [Name] is present and ready to fight!"**

**Check your work:** You should see 5 different lines in your console, one for each member of the squad from "Me" to "Pizza Guy."

## 🛡️ Mission: Gear Up the Squad
**The Mission:** A crate of armor just arrived! You need to give every person in your squad a shield. 

**The Goal:**
* Use a `for` loop to loop through the `squad` list.
* Inside the loop, update each name to include the word **" (Shielded)"** at the end. 
* *Hint: You’ll want to do something like `squad[i] = squad[i] + " (Shielded)";`*

**Check your work:** Print the `squad` list after the loop. It should look like: `["Me (Shielded)", "Bestie (Shielded)", ...]`

## 🔋 Mission: Robot’s Power Charge
**The Mission:** Your "Robot" teammate needs to charge up its battery before the big boss fight. It needs to count from 0 to 100, but it charges in jumps of **20**.

**The Goal:**
* Write a `for` loop where the variable `energy` starts at **0**.
* The loop should run as long as `energy` is **less than or equal to 100**.
* Instead of `i++`, use `energy += 20` to count by twenties.
* Inside the loop, print: **"Charging... [energy]%"**

**Check your work:** Your console should show 0%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, and 100%. If it stops at 80%, check your "less than or equal to" sign!
