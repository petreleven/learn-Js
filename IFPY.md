

## 🏎️ Mission: The Getaway Van (Python Edition)

**The Mission:**
Your squad found an old van, but you need to check the fuel gauge before you hit the gas! Create a variable called `fuelLevel` and set it to **45**.

Write a Python conditional statement that checks the fuel:

* **If** `fuelLevel` is more than **80** → Print: *"Full tank! Let's drive to the Secret Base."*
* **Elif** (else if) `fuelLevel` is more than **20** → Print: *"Running low. Stop at the gas station!"*
* **Else** → Print: *"Empty! Grab your running shoes!"*

**Check your work:**
Change the value of `fuelLevel` and make sure each condition works correctly.

## 🤖 Mission: Robot's Combat Mode (Python Edition)

**The Mission:**
Your "Robot" teammate has three modes depending on how many zombies are approaching. Create a variable called `zombieCount` and set it to **12**.

**Check your work:**
Test different values like 0, 5, and 20.

**The Mission:**
