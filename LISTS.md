## 🧟 The Zombie Survival Squad 🧟

**The Mission:** Start with a list containing `"Me"` and `"Bestie"`. Use the `.push()` method three separate times to add `"Scientist"`, `"Robot"`, and `"Pizza Guy"` to the end of your list.

**Check your work:** Display the entire list to make sure you have 5 survivors.

### 🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide:

* **Step 1:** Create a list called  `"friends"` list with `"Me"` and `"Bestie"`.
* **Step 2:** Apply the `.push()` method once to add the `"Scientist"`.
* **Step 3:** Apply the `.push()` method again to add the `"Robot"`.
* **Step 4:** Apply the `.push()` method a final time to add the `"Pizza Guy"`.
* **Step 5:** Display the final list of 5 survivors.

2. Use `.push()` to add a `"Cool Cape"` to the end.
3. Use the `[0]` index to store your shirt in a new variable.

**Check your work:** Verify the list has 3 items and the socks are gone.

### 🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide:

* **Step 1:** Apply `.splice(1, 1)` to the list to remove the item at index 1.
* **Step 2:** Apply `.push()` to add `"Cool Cape"` to the list.
