## Exercise 1: The "Bestie Squad" Profile Maker

**The Scenario:** You and your friends are starting a private social club, and you need a way to keep track of everyone’s profile stats in your app so you can organize meetups.

* **The Mission:** Create a `Friend` class to act as the "template" for every member of your squad.
* **The Info (Attributes):**
* `string name`: (e.g., "Maya", "Leo").
* `int friendshipLevel`: (A score from 1–100 of how well you know them).
* `bool isComingToParty`: (Whether they said yes to the weekend hangout).


* **The Constructor:** Write a constructor that fills in this info every time you add a new friend to your code.
* **The Fun Part:** In your `Main` method, create a few friends. Then, write an `if` statement that checks: "If `isComingToParty` is true, print 'Yay! [Name] is coming!'"

### Exercise 2: The "Outfit Planner" App

**The Scenario:** You have way too many clothes, and you keep forgetting what you own. You’re building a digital "Closet Manager" to help you pick your outfit for school or the weekend before you even open your wardrobe.

* **The Mission:** Create a `Clothing` class to save all your favorite pieces.
* **The Info (Attributes):**
* `string pieceName`: (e.g., "Sparkly Sneakers", "Graphic Hoodie").
* `string vibe`: (e.g., "Casual", "Fancy", "Sporty").
* `int comfortLevel`: (1–10).


* **The Constructor:** Create a constructor that locks in the name, vibe, and comfort level the moment you add a new item to your digital closet.
* **The Fun Part:** Add 3 items to a list in your `Program.cs`. Then, write a loop that prints out all the items that have a `comfortLevel` of 8 or higher, so you only see the comfiest stuff!
