About Al-Amin Glass & Aluminium

Overview

Founded in 2015, Al-amin Glass and Aluminium is a privately owned company located in Nairobi. We are direct importers of all types of float building glass and aluminium profiles. We do projects all over Kenya.


Scope

Our scope of work includes glazing of glass on steel windows with premium putty, aluminium doors & windows, office partitioning skylight roofs, balcony balustrades, frameless shower cubicles bathroom mirrors, window handles and stays, sandblasting and much more. Our projects range from residential houses, high-rise apartments and office blocks.


Vision

Enriching the construction industry and ensuring that we provide lasting satisfaction and trust to our stakeholders by continually importing, supplying and installing quality glass products.


Mission

To make Al-amin Glass and Aluminium a company that contributes value to its stakeholders.
