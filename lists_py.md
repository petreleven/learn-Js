## 🧟 The Zombie Survival Squad 🧟

**The Mission:** Chaos has broken out, and the undead are everywhere! You start with a Python list called `squad` that contains `"Me"` and `"Bestie"`. Suddenly, a group of three more survivors sprint up to your compound gate! Your task is to use Python's **`.append()`** method three separate times to lock `"Scientist"`, `"Robot"`, and `"Pizza Guy"` into the safety of your team list.

**Check your work:** Fire up the terminal using the `print()` command to make sure you have 5 total survivors ready to fight off the horde!

### 🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide:

* **Step 1:** Start with your initial squad list that already holds your starting duo: `"Me"` and `"Bestie"`.
* **Step 2:** Deploy the `.append()` method for the first time. Inside the parentheses, feed it the `"Scientist"` to load them into the back of the line.
* **Step 3:** Use `.append()` a second time, this time loading up the `"Robot"`.
* **Step 4:** Use `.append()` a third time to drag the `"Pizza Guy"` (and hopefully some pepperoni) into the compound.
* **Step 5:** Finally, use the `print()` command and put your squad's name inside the parentheses to summon your team of 5 to the screen!

## 🕹️ The Player One Power-Up 🍄

**The Mission:** You are hosting a massive gaming tournament and have a list called `players` with four legendary gamertags: `"Mario"`, `"Luigi"`, `"James"`, `"Lilian"`. Python lists are **zero-indexed**, meaning the computer counts the very first item as position **0**! Use the **`[0]`** bracket notation to snatch the name of the first-place player and lock it into a variable called `winner`.

**Check your work:** Flash a victory message on the screen that says `"The Golden Mushroom goes to: "` and automatically includes your `winner` variable.

### 🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide:

* **Step 1:** Scan your list of four players. Lock it into your brain that computer coding *always* starts counting at **0** instead of 1!
* **Step 2:** To grab the champion sitting in the very first slot ("Mario"), target the list using square brackets with the number `0` inside them: `[0]`.
* **Step 3:** Create a new data storage box (a variable) named `winner` and use the equals sign (`=`) to teleport that first player inside it.
* **Step 4:** Use Python's `print()` function to display your victory text. **Pro-Tip:** Put an `f` right before your quotation marks (`f"Text {variable}"`) to easily drop your `winner` variable inside curly brackets `{}`!

## 🕵️ The Double Agent Swap 🕶️

**The Mission:** You have a list of trusted heroes, but your radar detects a `"Spy"` hiding right at **index 2**. No fancy tools needed here—you can overwrite anyone in a Python list by targeting their index directly (like `list[index] = "New Item"`). Perform a secret override hack to replace the `"Spy"` with a `"Super Dog"`.

**Check your work:** Print your list to the screen. If the `"Spy"` has vanished and the `"Super Dog"` is standing guard in that middle spot, the mission is a success!

### 🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide:

* **Step 1:** Locate the target! The intel says they are hiding at index **2** (which is actually the *third* spot in the row because we start counting from 0: 0, 1, 2!).
* **Step 2:** Target that exact coordinate by writing the list's name followed immediately by square brackets: `[2]`.
* **Step 3:** Use the assignment operator (the equals sign `=`) to completely delete and overwrite whatever is currently in that slot.
* **Step 4:** Put `"Super Dog"` on the right side of the equals sign. This instantly kicks the spy out of the base and deploys the dog!
* **Step 5:** Use `print()` on your list to confirm the spy has been neutralized.

## 🎒 The Inventory Upgrade ⚔️

**The Mission:** Your character's loot inventory list is cluttered with trash: `["Sword", "Old Socks", "Shield"]`.

1. First, use Python's **`.remove()`** command to completely vaporize the `"Old Socks"`. *(In Python, `.remove("Item Name")` deletes an item by its actual name!)*
2. Next, use **`.append()`** to gear up by adding a `"Cool Cape"` to the very end of your inventory list.
3. Finally, use the **`[0]`** index to grab your primary weapon ("Sword") and store it in a variable.

**Check your work:** Print your final inventory. It should have exactly 3 epic items, and those smelly `"Old Socks"` should be completely wiped from the database!

### 🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide:

* **Step 1:** Vaporize the trash! Grab your inventory list and use the `.remove()` tool. Inside the parentheses, type `"Old Socks"` to delete them instantly.
* **Step 2:** Equip the rare loot! Use the `.append()` tool on your list to send a `"Cool Cape"` straight to your final inventory slot.
* **Step 3:** Choose your primary weapon. Since your weapon is at the very front of the inventory, target it using the `[0]` index.
* **Step 4:** Create a brand new variable with an awesome name like `equipped_weapon` and use the equals sign to store that sword inside it.
* **Step 5:** Use `print()` on your inventory list to make sure your character is geared up and ready for battle!
